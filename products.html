What it does

Everything a one-person business needs to track what it spends, bill what it is owed, and judge whether a property is worth buying.

Scan a receipt

Photograph a receipt and the amount is read off it automatically. Correct anything that looks wrong before saving — it is an ordinary editable field.

Track expenses

Categories you define, search, filters by type and year, running totals, and a CSV export that opens anywhere.

Send invoices

Line items, tax, your logo and your payment terms, rendered to a PDF you can print or share straight from your phone.

Deductible reports

Set a deductible percentage per category, pick a date range, and get the summary your accountant actually asks for.

Analyse a property

Cap rate, cash-on-cash, DSCR, net operating income and break-even occupancy — with a deal grade that shows exactly which assumption drove it.

Four languages

English, French, Spanish and German throughout, switchable in the app independently of your phone's language.
